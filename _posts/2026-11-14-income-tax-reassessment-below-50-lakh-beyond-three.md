title: "Income Tax | Reassessment Below ₹50 Lakh Beyond Three Years Invalid: Telangana HC"
date: 2026-11-14 00:59:24
layout: single
---

The High Court of Telangana has ruled that reassessment proceedings initiated under Section 148 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, are invalid and without jurisdiction if the alleged escaped income is below the statutory threshold of ₹50 lakh and the notice is issued beyond the three-year limitation period. Adjudicating on the post-Finance Act 2021 reassessment regime, the Court held that the Revenue cannot issue notices under Section 148 or 148A beyond the standard three-year window unless the jurisdictional condition precedent under Section 149(1)(b)—specifically that escaped income, represented in the form of an asset, expenditure, or entry, amounts to or is likely to amount to ₹50 lakh or more—is unequivocally satisfied.

This judgment reinforces the strict interpretation of statutory safeguards introduced under the amended reassessment framework, curbing arbitrary extensions of limitation by the tax authorities. By invalidating notices where the escaped income falls short of the ₹50 lakh benchmark for periods beyond three years from the end of the relevant assessment year, the ruling provides vital clarity and relief to taxpayers. For tax practitioners and assessees, this decision reaffirms that adherence to the monetary thresholds under Section 149 is a non-negotiable jurisdictional prerequisite, rendering any non-compliant reassessment order liable to be quashed at the threshold.
