title: "Income Tax | Section 148 Notices Beyond Surviving Time Invalid: ITAT Rajkot"
date: 2026-11-18 07:50:11
layout: single
---

The Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT), Rajkot Bench, has held that reassessment notices issued under Section 148 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, beyond the permissible "surviving time limit" are legally invalid and void *ab initio*. Navigating the procedural framework established post-Finance Act, 2021, and the transitional provisions under the Taxation and Other Laws (Relaxation and Amendment of Certain Provisions) Act (TOLA), the Tribunal reaffirmed that the Revenue must strictly adhere to statutory limitation periods. In cases where the Assessing Officer fails to issue the final reassessment notice within the balance of time available—after accounting for statutory exclusions such as the response window under Section 148A(b)—the Revenue forfeits its jurisdiction, rendering any consequential reassessment proceedings non-est in the eyes of the law.

This ruling reinforces a critical defense for taxpayers challenging the jurisdictional validity of delayed reassessment proceedings. By clarifying that administrative relaxations cannot indefinitely extend the lifespan of limitation provisions under Section 149, the decision underscores the principle that statutory time limits are mandatory, not discretionary. For tax professionals and corporate assessees, this precedent serves as a robust benchmark to scrutinize the timeline of Section 148 notices, ensuring that assessments revived or initiated without statutory authority can be effectively challenged on threshold jurisdictional grounds.
