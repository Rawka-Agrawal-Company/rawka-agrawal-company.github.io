Established in 1979, **Rawka Agrawal & Company** is a reputed Chartered Accountancy firm headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh[cite: 7]. With over four decades of professional excellence, the firm operates in strategic alliance with **Kailash C. Agrawal & Company**, combining expertise to provide seamless, specialized services to listed entities, private companies, LLPs, trusts, and high-net-worth individuals[cite: 7].

The group files over 950 income tax returns annually, conducts 175+ audits, and handles complex search and assessment matters under Income Tax laws[cite: 7].
