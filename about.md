Established in 1979, **Rawka Agrawal & Company** is a reputed Chartered Accountancy firm headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. With over four decades of professional excellence, the firm operates in strategic alliance with **Kailash C. Agrawal & Company**, combining expertise to provide seamless, specialized services to listed entities, private companies, LLPs, trusts, and high-net-worth individuals.

The group files over 950 income tax returns annually, conducts 175+ audits, and handles complex search and assessment matters under Income Tax laws.

* **Adv. Soumya Garg (Legal Associate):** Specializes in NCLT matters, Tax litigation, and Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC).
